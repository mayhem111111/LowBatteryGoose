# Low Battery Goose ($LBG): launch marketing strategy

> **Internal proposal only:** The Flock Drop figures below are not proof that the campaign is approved, configured, or funded. Do not announce it as live until the founder approves final rules, a real quest URL exists, and a disclosed wallet is funded. Verify every advertised token prize before describing it as available.


Launch: Pump.fun, Nov 4, 2026, 6:00 PM PT. Owner: Jason (dev). Community lead: Tony.
SOL/USD $96.61 (Crypto.com, 2026-11-04 16:59 UTC). Pump.fun figures are estimates from the standard curve; the create screen is authoritative.

## 1. Positioning (what every post says)
**One line:** Low Battery Goose is a community meme for everyone running on 1%: free games, weekly contests,
and a flock that votes on what gets built next.

**Say:** community, free games, contests, rewards for taking part, transparent team wallets, locked team tokens, "be early to the flock".
**Never say:** "100x", "moon", "guaranteed", "profit", "spectacular opportunity", "get rich", dollar values of rewards, "presale", "burn".
Reason: profit language turns a meme community into something that looks like an investment offer, and it's
the #1 thing scam coins say. Trust is the edge here, so the copy has to look nothing like a scam.

## 2. The Flock Drop (airdrop program)
| | |
|---|---|
| Pool | **10,000,000 LBG (1% of supply)**, bought in the founder's first buy (+≈0.33 SOL). Held in a disclosed "Flock Drop" wallet, never mixed with team wallets |
| Season 1 | Launch night → 14 days later (Nov 18) |
| Base reward | **5,000 LBG**: join the Telegram, follow @LowBatteryGoose, submit a Solana wallet on the quest board, pass the bot check. First 1,000 verified members (5,000,000 LBG) |
| Bonus quests | +2,500 LBG: post your Honk Bingo badge · +2,500 LBG: enter the meme contest · +1,000 LBG per friend who completes the base quest (max 5) |
| Max per person | 15,000 LBG |
| Bonus pool | 5,000,000 LBG; quests close when it runs out |
| Payout | One batch after Season 1 ends and the bot review is done, with the transaction published |
| Rules | Free: no purchase or holding required. One per person. Team excluded. Bots and duplicate wallets removed. Void where prohibited. Rewards have no promised value |

Why this design: base plus quests rewards real participation, not wallet farming. A single delayed payout
after a bot review stops "claim and dump" on launch night. And because no purchase is required, it stays a
free promotion, not a lottery.

**Run it on a quest platform** (Zealy, Galxe or TaskOn). They handle sign-ups, wallet collection, X and
Telegram verification and bot filtering. Set it up before 6 PM; it takes about 30 minutes.
