Governance Board

The five proposed Board offices oversee governance, community representation, records, and accountability. The Board does not independently control or custody the treasury.

1. Governance Chair

Coordinates governance and meetings; ensures rules are followed without unilateral financial authority.

2. Deputy Governance Chair

Supports the Chair and continuity, process quality, and accountability.

3. Governance Secretary

Records proposals, meetings, decisions, approvals, and public governance reports.

4. Treasury and Accountability Representative

Monitors budgets and funding reports and oversees compliance with the two-signature policy. No unilateral custody or spending power.

5. Community Representative

Brings member feedback, concerns, and proposals to the Board.

How we intend to operate

  • Written role descriptions and 30/60/90-day objectives.
  • Published proposals, meeting records, votes, action owners, and progress reports.
  • Conflict-of-interest disclosures and recusals.
  • Clear separation between governance oversight and treasury custody.
  • Two independent authorized signatures for every project-controlled asset release.
  • No claim of active DAO voting or operational multisig until implemented and independently verified.
