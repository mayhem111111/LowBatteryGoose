03 / UNIVERSAL TWO-SIGNATURE RULE
NO SINGLE-PERSON RELEASE AUTHORITY

Two signatures.
Every release. Every time.

Nothing under project control is released without at least two independent authorized signatures. This rule applies across the entire Low Battery Goose community and ecosystem, regardless of role, title, department, allocation, or asset type.

  • Applies to project-controlled tokens, SOL, treasury funds, liquidity assets while controlled by the project, reserves, grants, rewards, prizes, marketing and production budgets, games, staking or vesting distributions, airdrops, and cross-chain reserves.
  • Applies to founder and developer allocations while held or administered by project-controlled wallets, as well as governance and departmental funds.
  • No founder, governance chair, committee head, developer, department lead, administrator, or other individual may authorize a release alone.
  • Governance approval, a job title, an emergency label, or a verbal instruction does not replace the second independent signature.
  • Every release must have a documented purpose, asset and quantity, recipient address, budget or program reference, supporting records, two approvals, and a publicly verifiable transaction record where the network provides one.

The Head of the Governance Committee oversees adherence to the policy but does not receive unilateral custody or release authority. The policy is intended to be enforced through a properly configured multisignature wallet or equivalent technical controls. The live signer set, threshold, wallet address, and operational status must be published only after they are established and independently verified.

Scope and honest disclosure: This policy governs assets held, controlled, or administered by the project. It cannot prevent an independent token holder from transferring assets held in that person's own self-custody wallet. Until the multisig configuration is verified, this page describes the required policy—not a claim that technical enforcement is already active.

04 / ACCOUNTABILITY & COMMUNITY

Show the work.
Show the receipts.

The security and transparency program is intended to maintain an auditable record of project-controlled assets and activity. Records should include incoming contributions, wallet balances, approvals, transactions, program budgets, recipients, milestones, and reconciliation notes. Public reporting should protect personal information and operational security while allowing the community to understand where funds came from and where they went.

Community members may contribute skills across video, editing, graphics, writing, presenting, education, social media, development, AI learning, games, marketing, events, and moderation. Participation does not automatically create compensation or token entitlement: work, deliverables, budget, eligibility, and approvals must be defined before funds are committed.

05 / IMPORTANT DISCLOSURES

Know what is planned
and what is live.

Low Battery Goose is a community project with a planned Solana-first token and a longer-term cross-chain ecosystem vision. Cross-chain services, bridges, staking, vesting, rewards, and other utilities must not be treated as live until they are built, reviewed, funded, and publicly confirmed. No investment return, token value, liquidity outcome, reward, or yield is guaranteed.

Before relying on any token address, supply, allocation, purchase, or treasury balance, verify the relevant on-chain data and transaction signature. Never share a seed phrase, private key, password, or recovery code with anyone claiming to represent the project.
