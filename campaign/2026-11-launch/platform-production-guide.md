# Platform production guide (2026-11-05 review)

**This is an organic-community production guide, not clearance to buy ads.** Platform features, account eligibility and media limits change. Check the live composer and linked platform documentation immediately before export/publish. Crypto-related paid promotion can face country-, product- and account-specific restrictions; do not run paid campaigns without platform approval and legal review.

## Platform-specific copy approach

- **X:** one strong hook, one idea, official-channel CTA; thread only when useful.
- **Telegram:** direct operational updates, clear admin safety reminder, one official source link.
- **Instagram:** visual-first caption; invite a genuine story or remix.
- **TikTok:** hook in first 1–2 seconds, visible captions, original sound/voiceover.
- **Facebook:** community question and accessible image description.
- **Reddit:** contribute a useful/funny story or free game; no drive-by token shill.
- **Discord:** clear announcement, one action, one source of truth; avoid repeated pings.
- **YouTube Shorts:** visual gag and payoff; CTA in spoken/caption text.
- **Threads:** conversational observation and a specific question.
- **LinkedIn:** frame as a community creativity project, not an investment pitch.
- **Group chats:** only where welcomed; ask before sharing.

## Production rules

- Export each aspect ratio as its own composition; do not stretch or crop one master blindly.
- Keep logos and essential text inside central safe zones; test on a phone.
- Add burned-in captions to short-form video and provide alt text for static artwork.
- Use original goose artwork; do not lift creator art or copyrighted meme templates.
- Remove price charts, profit language, urgency-to-buy language and unverified token-address overlays.
- Keep all prelaunch artwork address-free. LIVE artwork should link to the founder's verified announcement rather than embedding a manually retyped address.
