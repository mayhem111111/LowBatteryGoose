Roadmap

A staged roadmap for the Low Battery Goose community, games, rewards, and future Solana integrations.

Phase 1 — Launch verification

Confirm the Pump.fun mint, publish the contract address, reconcile Founder and Doobie purchases, publish tokenomics, and disclose the treasury/multisig wallet and signer policy.

Phase 2 — Games and community rewards

Improve the existing browser games, publish transparent game rules, and design airdrop and prize-pool eligibility with anti-bot review and public reporting. Rewards are not active until funded and announced.

Phase 3 — Treasury and governance

Publish treasury controls, the two-signer release process, allocation reporting, and governance procedures before accepting or distributing community funds.

Phase 4 — Staking and ecosystem utilities

Research and, only after security review and funding, build any staking or additional token utility. No staking contract, yield, or investment product is live or promised.
