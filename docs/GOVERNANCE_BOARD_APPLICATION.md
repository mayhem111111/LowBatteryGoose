## Governance and integrity commitments

Applicants should be willing to:

- Act respectfully, transparently, and in the interests of the wider community.
- Disclose relevant conflicts of interest and recuse themselves where appropriate.
- Keep accurate records of proposals, discussions, decisions, and assigned actions.
- Avoid promising token returns, guaranteed rewards, allocations, or outcomes.
- Respect privacy and never request another person's seed phrase, private key, password, or signing access.
- Support independent review and clear public reporting.
- Follow the project's two-independent-signature policy for every project-controlled asset release.

**Treasury safeguard:** A council title does not grant custody or unilateral spending authority. No chair, committee lead, developer, administrator, or individual may approve a project-controlled release alone. Any release requires two independent authorized signatures and a documented purpose, amount, recipient, approval record, and transaction record where available. The policy is not proof that a multisig is already operational.

## Selection process — proposed

1. Applications are collected and reviewed against published role criteria.
2. Shortlisted applicants may be contacted for a short discussion.
3. The project publishes the selection method, conflicts policy, seat count, term length, and any community ratification or voting procedure before appointments are finalized.
4. Selected members, responsibilities, term dates, and any changes are announced through official project channels.

No applicant is appointed merely by submitting this form. Do not imply that token-weighted voting, an on-chain DAO, a Realm, or binding governance is live until the relevant system has been implemented and verified.
