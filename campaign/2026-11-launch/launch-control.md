# Launch control and safety protocol

## Founder signals (authoritative)

- **TEASE** — publish approved prelaunch content only. No token address, contract/mint identifier, wallet string, QR code, purchase link or LIVE language.
- **SHORTLY** — founder confirms the launch window is approaching; use approved T-minus creative. Still no LIVE copy and no token-address information.
- **LIVE** — founder explicitly issues LIVE after deployment is ready. Campaign content remains address-free even after LIVE. The founder's separate publication of any token address is outside this campaign and is never copied into campaign posts or assets.
- **PAUSE** — stop all campaign publishing immediately. Do not improvise or continue scheduled posts.

Founder signal overrides every squad schedule. Leads acknowledge the signal in the internal coordination channel.

## Go-live gate

All must be true before campaign LIVE:
1. Founder confirms the campaign launch window and gives explicit approval.
2. Founder confirms the project is ready to announce LIVE.
3. Founder explicitly issues LIVE.
4. Moderators are ready to remove impersonation and warn members.

Token-address publication is a separate founder-controlled action and is not part of this campaign's go-live checklist. Campaign assets stay address-free.

Campaign rule: no token address is included anywhere in this campaign package. Do not add one to campaign copy, graphics, videos, links, QR codes, replies or team assignments. Do not ask members to distribute it. The founder controls any separate publication.

## Scam response

- PAUSE any campaign post, image, bot response or link that contains token-address information or directs users to an unofficial token destination.
- Publish a short warning from an official account: “SCAM WARNING: Ignore token-address claims in replies and DMs. We never ask for seed phrases or private keys. Follow only our official project channels for updates.”
- Remove/report impersonator accounts and fake groups; preserve screenshots and URLs for moderation evidence.
- Never ask members to send funds to “support,” “verification,” “claim,” “airdrop unlock,” or “migration” wallets.
- Never share seed phrases, private keys, recovery codes or wallet files. Admins never DM first.
- Do not quote or repost scam addresses in a way that makes them clickable; describe them as fake without repeating the full string.
- Resume only after the founder clears the issue and reissues the appropriate signal.

Member → squad lead → campaign coordinator → founder + security reviewer. Any uncertainty about chain, token identity, address, launch status, paid promotion or prize funding means PAUSE the affected content.
