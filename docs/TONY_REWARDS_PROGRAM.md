# Pass the Goose: community rewards program (run by Tony)

Different from the Flock Drop (Jason's one-time sign-up airdrop). Pass the Goose is **ongoing, content-driven
and built to spread**: people make a goose meme, tag friends to "take the goose", and climb Flock Ranks.
Rewards are recognition first, prizes second. Free to enter, judged by the community, never random, never purchase-based.

## 1. How it spreads
1. Make your **Battery Check** meme on lowbatterygoose.pages.dev (pick your %, write what drained you).
2. Post it on X with **#PassTheGoose** and tag **3 friends**: "🪿 I'm at 3%. You've been goosed. Pass it on."
3. Each tagged friend makes their own and tags 3 more. One post can reach dozens of new people in a day,
   and every post carries the website link and the goose.
4. Share your post in t.me/LowBatteryFlock so it counts.

## 2. Flock Ranks (earned by activity, not by holding tokens)
| Rank | How to earn it | What you get |
|---|---|---|
| 🥚 Egg | Post 1 #PassTheGoose meme | "Egg" title in Telegram |
| 🐣 Gosling | 5 memes, or 3 friends who passed the goose from your tag | Gosling role + shoutout in the weekly recap |
| 🪿 Honker | 15 memes, or 10 friends in your chain | Honker role, early access to new Honk Arcade games |
| 🏆 Golden Goose | Top 10 of the month by community votes | Custom goose art of you, name in the Hall of Honks, can nominate a Flock Council candidate |

Tony tracks ranks in a simple spreadsheet from Telegram shares and #PassTheGoose posts.

## 3. Weekly prizes (every Sunday)
| Prize | Picked by | Reward |
|---|---|---|
| Funniest Goose of the Week | Telegram poll (top 5 shortlisted by Tony) | [WEEKLY_PRIZE_1] + featured on X |
| Longest Goose Chain | Count of friends who joined via your tags | [WEEKLY_PRIZE_2] |
| Best Original Art | Community vote | [WEEKLY_PRIZE_3] + art on the website |
| Rising Gosling (new member) | Tony's pick | Custom goose avatar |

Prize amounts: set a fixed weekly budget the team can always pay (for example a fixed amount of SOL or LBG
from the team's creator fees). Announce the amounts each Monday and post the payout transactions each Sunday.
Never promise more than the budget, and never quote dollar values for LBG prizes.

## 4. Special events (keep it fresh)
- **1% Monday:** everyone posts their lowest battery of the week.
- **Goose Court Wednesday:** community votes on a silly debate; winners get a role for the week.
- **Honk Bingo Friday:** fastest full-board screenshot posted in the group.
- **Milestone parties:** at every 500 new Telegram members, a 1-hour meme blitz with double prizes that week.

## 5. Rules (paste into the Telegram pin)
```
🪿 PASS THE GOOSE: RULES
• Free to enter. No purchase or token holding required.
• Original memes only. No hate, no NSFW, no stolen art.
• Winners are picked by community vote, not luck.
• One account per person. Bots and fake accounts are removed and lose their rank.
• Prizes are paid by the team from a fixed weekly budget, announced every Monday.
• Admins never DM first. Never share your seed phrase.
```

## 7. Tony's daily routine (30–45 minutes)
1. Morning: post the event of the day (1% Monday, Goose Court and so on) on X and in Telegram.
2. Midday: repost the 5 best #PassTheGoose memes from X; reply to every new member.
3. Evening: update the rank sheet; give out new roles in Telegram.
4. Sunday: run the vote, announce winners, post the payout transactions, Monday's prize amounts and the weekly recap.
