THE FLOCK'S GAME PLAN · COMMUNITY REVIEW EDITION

Big honks.
Real progress.

A staged plan to grow Low Battery Goose from a playful Solana-first community into a useful, creative, education-led ecosystem. We build in public, protect community resources, and move forward when each stage is ready—not just because a calendar says so.

PHASE 00
🧭
FOUNDATION & DISCLOSURE

Get the nest in order

Establish the public project foundation and make sure the community can distinguish confirmed facts from plans.

  • Maintain official website, social links, project identity, and public document hub.
  • Publish tokenomics, roadmap, risk disclosures, and a verification register.
  • Verify the mint, supply, wallet ownership/control, purchases, and transaction signatures before publishing them as completed facts.
  • Document governance roles, decision procedures, and conflicts-of-interest expectations.
PHASE 01
🛡️
SECURITY & TREASURY CONTROLS

Two keys. One standard.

Make transparency and accountability the operating standard across the entire project-controlled ecosystem.

  • Establish a multisignature wallet or equivalent technical enforcement with at least two independent signers.
  • Apply the two-signature authorization rule to every project-controlled release, transfer, withdrawal, distribution, and disbursement.
  • Publish verified signer threshold, wallet addresses, treasury controls, approval records, and reporting practices once operational.
  • Maintain an auditable record of budgets, recipients, transaction signatures, balances, and reconciliations.
PHASE 02
🪿
COMMUNITY & PARTICIPATION

Make room for every honker

Build a welcoming community where people can contribute their talents and help shape the direction of the project.

  • Create clear participation pathways for moderation, events, games, education, design, writing, marketing, and technical work.
  • Invite community members to showcase skills in video, editing, graphics, presenting, development, AI learning, and content creation.
  • Define deliverables, eligibility, budgets, and approval processes before any paid work or token-funded contribution.
  • Publish community proposals, decisions, and progress updates in an accessible format.
PHASE 03
🎮
GAMES & EXPERIENCES

Play together. Build together.

Expand the browser-based game experience and create community activities that are fun, fair, and easy to understand.

  • Improve 1% Honk Bingo and other existing game experiences.
  • Explore new casual games, seasonal challenges, leaderboards, and community events.
  • Publish rules, scoring, eligibility, prize terms, and selection methods before each event.
  • Keep games free to access where practical and never require a wallet connection merely to play a free game.
PHASE 04
🎥
MEDIA & VIDEOGRAPHY

Lights, camera, honk!

Build a dedicated media and videography program that helps the community learn, share, and tell its stories.

  • Develop a YouTube presence with platform explainers, project specials, interviews, demonstrations, and community features.
  • Produce accessible education on crypto basics, blockchain concepts, wallets, transactions, and common security threats.
  • Explore AI education and practical demonstrations for people who want to understand and use emerging tools.
  • Provide ways for community members to contribute as presenters, writers, camera operators, editors, designers, and producers.
PHASE 05
🧠
EDUCATION & SUPPORT

Questions welcome. Scams aren't.

Help community members build confidence through practical, approachable learning and security awareness.

  • Develop a community knowledge hub and question-and-answer content.
  • Train designated team contributors to explain general crypto, AI, and wallet-safety concepts.
  • Teach safe habits: verify official links, check addresses, understand approvals, and protect recovery phrases.
  • Never request seed phrases, private keys, passwords, recovery codes, or remote wallet access.
PHASE 06
🎁
REWARDS & COMMUNITY PROGRAMS

Make participation meaningful

Consider transparent, sustainable programs that recognize useful contributions and community participation.

  • Design any rewards, airdrops, prizes, or incentive programs with clear eligibility, funding, claim windows, and anti-abuse rules.
  • Publish budgets and selection methods before a program begins.
  • Require two independent signatures before any project-controlled reward or prize is released.
  • Do not imply that rewards are active, guaranteed, or an investment return until a funded program is formally launched.
PHASE 07
🔧
UTILITY & FOUNDATIONAL SERVICES

Build things people can use

Research and develop useful community-led services that fit the project's capabilities and long-term sustainability.

  • Evaluate tools, services, partnerships, and digital experiences that solve real community problems.
  • Publish scope, costs, security assumptions, and maintenance responsibilities before launch.
  • Build incrementally and test before exposing community funds or users to new systems.
PHASE 08
🌉
CROSS-CHAIN RESEARCH

Think beyond one chain

Explore a broader cross-chain future while keeping Solana as the initial foundation.

  • Research interoperability options, bridge risks, custody models, and supported networks.
  • Require independent security review and a clear operational plan before any bridge or cross-chain reserve is used.
  • Disclose supported networks and actual deployed functionality only after implementation and verification.
PHASE 09
♾️
LONG-TERM SUSTAINABILITY

Keep the flock flying

Build a community that can adapt, improve, and continue responsibly over time.

  • Review programs regularly and publish what is working, what is changing, and what has been retired.
  • Set clear budgets, eligibility, start and end dates, renewal criteria, and closeout procedures for individual programs.
  • Use community feedback and transparent governance to guide future priorities.
  • Prioritize security, accountability, realistic delivery, and responsible use of resources.
THE RULE THAT RUNS THROUGH EVERY PHASE
UNIVERSAL TWO-SIGNATURE AUTHORIZATION

No asset moves
on one person's say-so.

At least two independent authorized signatures are required before any project-controlled token or fund is released, transferred, withdrawn, distributed, or disbursed. This includes the founder, developers, governance program, every department, and all project-controlled community programs. No title or committee role creates a single-person exception.

Every release must be documented and traceable. Governance oversight and approval do not replace the second signature. The technical multisig setup must be verified before the project claims the rule is technically enforced. The policy applies to project-controlled assets; it cannot restrict independent holders' self-custodied tokens.

A NOTE ON TIMING

Milestones, not magic dates.

This roadmap describes intended direction, not guaranteed delivery dates. Each phase depends on resources, technical readiness, security review, community decisions, and transparent funding. Features and programs may be revised, delayed, paused, or cancelled when that is the responsible choice. Any active program must publish its own rules and status.

Not financial advice: LBG is a speculative crypto asset. Nothing in this roadmap guarantees token value, liquidity, income, yield, rewards, or future performance. Cross-chain integrations, staking, vesting, and other utilities are goals for research and development—not claims that they are currently live.
