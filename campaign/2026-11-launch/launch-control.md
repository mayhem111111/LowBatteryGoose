# Launch control and safety protocol

## Founder signals (authoritative)

- **TEASE** — publish approved prelaunch content only. No address, purchase link or LIVE language.
- **SHORTLY** — founder confirms the launch window is approaching; use approved T-minus creative. Still no LIVE copy.
- **LIVE** — may be issued only after actual deployment, chain/token identity verification, official address verification, founder publication of the official announcement, and founder's explicit LIVE signal.
- **PAUSE** — stop all campaign publishing immediately. Do not improvise or continue scheduled posts.

Founder signal overrides every squad schedule. Leads acknowledge the signal in the internal coordination channel.

## Go-live gate

All must be true before LIVE:
1. The deployed chain and launch mechanism are confirmed from actual deployment evidence.
2. Token name/ticker and token identity are verified on the chain's authoritative explorer.
3. Official address is copied from the actual deployment/official launch page and independently checked by a second authorized reviewer.
4. Founder publishes the official announcement on the project's official channel.
5. Founder explicitly issues LIVE.
6. Website, bot and official social links show the same verified address and destination.
7. Moderators are ready to remove impersonation and warn members.

**Current repository state does not pass this gate.** The public site config still has a null mint and Pump.fun link. Repository planning documents conflict on launch path, and the campaign brief introduces TON as a possible current direction. No address is present in this control document by design.

## Scam response

- PAUSE any post, image, bot response or link that contains an address not copied from the verified official announcement.
- Publish a short warning from an official account: “SCAM WARNING: Ignore DMs and unofficial addresses. We never ask for seed phrases or private keys. Use only the address linked in our pinned official announcement.”
- Remove/report impersonator accounts and fake groups; preserve screenshots and URLs for moderation evidence.
- Never ask members to send funds to “support,” “verification,” “claim,” “airdrop unlock,” or “migration” wallets.
- Never share seed phrases, private keys, recovery codes or wallet files. Admins never DM first.
- Do not quote or repost scam addresses in a way that makes them clickable; describe them as fake without repeating the full string.
- Resume only after the founder clears the issue and reissues the appropriate signal.

Replace the bracket only with the actual founder announcement URL. Do not manually type an address into derivative posts or graphics.

## Internal escalation

Member → squad lead → campaign coordinator → founder + security reviewer. Any uncertainty about chain, token identity, address, launch status, paid promotion or prize funding means PAUSE the affected content.
