# 48-hour campaign runbook

> THE FLOCK IS GETTING READY. LOW BATTERY GOOSE ($LBG) IS LAUNCHING WITHIN 48 HOURS. THE GOOSE IS LOOSE.

Variants:
1. The flock has entered its 48-hour battery warning. LOW BATTERY GOOSE ($LBG) is preparing to launch. Follow the official channels; details will come from the founder.
2. 1% battery. 100% honk. LOW BATTERY GOOSE ($LBG) is getting ready. Launch is within 48 hours.
3. The goose has checked the calendar. The flock has checked the group chat. LOW BATTERY GOOSE ($LBG): within 48 hours.
4. Emergency flock bulletin: honk reserves critically low. LOW BATTERY GOOSE ($LBG) is preparing for launch within 48 hours.
5. A goose-shaped disturbance is approaching the internet. Follow LOW BATTERY GOOSE ($LBG) for the founder's verified launch update.

Do not add a chain, platform, address, price, exchange, allocation, utility or launch clock time unless verified and approved.

## 3. Squad operations

Eight squads of ten: A=01–10, B=11–20, C=21–30, D=31–40, E=41–50, F=51–60, G=61–70, H=71–80.

Each member receives one unique CSV assignment containing:
- primary announcement and alternate caption;
- original meme visual concept;
- short-form video concept;
- genuine reply assignment;
- community invitation;
- posting instruction.

Squad leads:
- distribute assignments privately and confirm receipt;
- ensure members use their own voice and original media;
- schedule staggered windows (not synchronized blasts);
- check the destination community permits promotion;
- collect public post links and metrics;
- stop or correct content when PAUSE is issued.

No member is required to post on every platform. Match each creative to the platform and audience.

## 4. Recommended stagger

Within each phase, rotate a small number of members at a time rather than publishing all 80 posts simultaneously. Suggested starting pattern: two members per squad in each 30-minute window, with the remaining members using original replies, community challenges, or later windows. Never manufacture replies or coordinate artificial likes/reposts.

## 5. Engagement and reporting

Record one row per published asset:
participant_id, squad, platform, post_url, publish_time_with_timezone, content_type, original_or_adapted, impressions/views, replies/comments, reposts/shares, saves, link clicks (if available), genuine new community members, referral source, moderation action, notes.

Report at T-24h, T-12h, T-60m, T+1h and T+24h. Compare meaningful replies, shares, saves, qualified joins and referral conversions—not likes alone. Do not claim a conversion unless it is actually attributed.
