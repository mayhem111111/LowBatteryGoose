# Low Battery Goose — Governance Board Operating Charter (Proposed)

> **Status: proposed interim framework.** This document is a draft for community review. It does not establish a legal entity, active DAO, on-chain voting system, binding election, or currently appointed board. It becomes effective only after the project publishes an approval notice, effective date, named initial members, and any required community ratification.

## 1. Purpose and authority

The Governance Board exists to represent community interests, maintain fair and transparent governance, set and review organizational policy, oversee accountability, and publish clear records. It does not independently own, custody, access, or control the treasury, and it cannot bypass the project's two-independent-signature rule.

The Board may:
- Set governance agendas and maintain proposal procedures.
- Review and recommend policies, priorities, community programs, and organizational standards.
- Request financial and operational reports and publish oversight findings.
- Appoint working groups within an approved scope and budget.
- Recommend actions to the project owner or authorized operators where implementation authority remains outside the Board.

The Board may not:
- Sign, transfer, withdraw, or distribute project assets solely by virtue of Board office.
- Give any single member unilateral spending authority or privileged wallet access.
- Represent a recommendation as executed until an authorized operator confirms execution.
- Promise investment returns, token price performance, allocations, or rewards.
- Claim an on-chain vote, DAO, multisig, or legal fiduciary status exists unless independently verified.

## 2. Composition and terms

The proposed Board has five distinct offices:
1. Governance Chair
2. Deputy Governance Chair
3. Governance Secretary
4. Treasury and Accountability Representative
5. Community Representative

Proposed initial term: 12 months, with a 60-day onboarding and review period. No person may hold more than one voting Board office at a time. A vacancy is filled through the same published appointment process, for the remainder of the term. These details require approval before appointments.

The Community and Operations Team roles are not Board seats and do not automatically carry Board votes, treasury access, or authority to approve expenditure.

## 3. Meetings, quorum, and voting

- Hold at least one scheduled Board meeting each month; publish the agenda at least 72 hours in advance except for documented urgent matters.
- Quorum is three of the five seated, eligible, non-recused Board members.
- Each seated Board member has one vote. No token-weighted or wallet-weighted voting is implied.
- Ordinary Board recommendations pass by a simple majority of votes cast at a quorate meeting. Abstentions are recorded and are not counted as votes cast.
- A tie means the motion does not pass; the Chair has no casting vote.
- Changes to this Charter, removal of an elected/appointed Board member, or changes to treasury safeguards require at least four affirmative votes from eligible Board members and a separately announced community ratification process before taking effect.
- Votes may be conducted asynchronously only when the proposal, discussion window, deadline, eligible voters, and recorded result are public and auditable.
- Every decision record states the motion, proposer, date, quorum, votes for/against/abstain, recusals, rationale, owner, deadline, and status.

These are proposed rules and must be ratified before being represented as binding.

## 4. Proposal lifecycle

1. **Submission:** Any community member may submit a proposal using the published process. A proposal must state the problem, requested decision, rationale, scope, expected cost (if any), risks, conflicts, implementation owner, and success measure.
2. **Screening:** The Secretary checks completeness and assigns a public proposal ID. Incomplete or unsafe proposals are returned with reasons.
3. **Disclosure:** Proposers and reviewers disclose relevant financial, personal, or organizational conflicts.
4. **Discussion:** Publish the proposal and allow at least five calendar days for community comment. Urgent exceptions require written justification.
5. **Board review:** The Board may amend, approve, reject, defer, or recommend community consideration. Material amendments restart the comment period.
6. **Vote:** Publish the exact motion, eligible voters, quorum, voting window, and method before voting opens. Keep a durable record.
7. **Decision and implementation:** The Secretary publishes the result. An implementation owner accepts or declines the assignment and reports progress. Board approval alone does not execute treasury or technical actions.
8. **Closeout:** Publish the outcome, evidence of completion, deviations, and lessons learned.

## 5. Appointments and removal

- Publish role descriptions, seat count, eligibility, term, time commitment, selection criteria, compensation status, conflict rules, and removal process before accepting applications.
- Applications are reviewed by a temporary selection panel of at least three reviewers who disclose conflicts and recuse where necessary. Candidates are scored against a published rubric: role competence (30%), relevant experience/work samples (25%), reliability and time commitment (20%), judgment/security awareness (15%), and community conduct (10%).
- Publish a shortlist and concise selection rationale, subject to applicant privacy. Provide a fair opportunity for candidate questions.
- Initial appointments are provisional until the selection outcome and any required community ratification are published.
- The proposed Board selection method is community ratification of the selection panel's shortlist by a one-person/one-vote community poll with a minimum seven-day window. The poll is advisory until a verified identity/eligibility and anti-manipulation process exists; do not call an informal poll binding.
- Members may resign in writing. Removal for misconduct, persistent non-performance, undisclosed conflict, security breach, or abuse of authority requires notice of the concern, an opportunity to respond, recusal of conflicted decision-makers, a documented Board vote, and community review/ratification. Immediate temporary suspension of access may be used to contain a credible security risk, but is not a final finding of misconduct.
- Operations Team appointments are made against role-specific work plans by the designated team lead and are reviewed at least quarterly. They do not require Board membership.

## 6. Accountability and performance

Every Board and Operations Team member receives a written role description, decision boundaries, reporting line, expected time commitment, and 30/60/90-day objectives.

- Monthly public report: meetings held, proposals received and status, decisions, attendance, deliverables, open risks, conflicts/recusals, and overdue actions.
- Quarterly role review: deliverables completed, quality, responsiveness, conduct, time commitment, and community feedback.
- Maintain a public action register with owner, due date, status, and evidence of completion.
- Publish corrections when prior reports are inaccurate. Preserve an audit trail rather than silently overwriting decisions.
- Provide a confidential route to report harassment, fraud, security issues, or conflicts; limit access to those who need to investigate.
- No retaliation for good-faith reporting or dissent.

## 7. Treasury independence and financial controls

The Board provides oversight, not unilateral treasury control. The Treasury and Accountability Representative reviews and reports on budgets, transaction records, and approved funding decisions; the representative does not gain custody or signing authority solely from the office.

Every project-controlled release, transfer, withdrawal, distribution, or disbursement must require **two independent authorized signatures**. Each proposed transaction must have a documented purpose, amount, recipient, approval record, and transaction reference where available. No chair, deputy, committee lead, treasury representative, developer, administrator, or other individual may authorize a release alone.

Board policy approval does not substitute for transaction authorization. Signers must be separately appointed under a documented custody policy and technically enforced by a verified multisig or equivalent control. Until wallet address, signer identities, threshold, recovery process, and on-chain enforcement are independently checked, describe the two-signature requirement as policy—not as an operational multisig.

Publish periodic treasury reports with appropriate privacy/security redactions: opening and closing balances, inflows/outflows, categories, approved budgets, transaction links where safe, and variances. Never publish seed phrases, private keys, passwords, or sensitive signer details.

## 8. Conflicts, conduct, and security

Members disclose relevant conflicts on appointment and whenever circumstances change. A conflicted member does not participate in deliberation or voting on the affected matter; the Secretary records the disclosure and recusal. Apply a published code of conduct to harassment, discrimination, intimidation, spam, impersonation, and misuse of community information.

No member may request or retain another person's seed phrase, private key, recovery phrase, password, or signing credential. Use least-privilege access, individual accounts, multi-factor authentication where available, and prompt access revocation when a role ends.

## 9. Transparency and privacy

Publish agendas, minutes, proposals, votes, reports, role descriptions, and appointment outcomes. Redact personal contact information, private applicant responses, security-sensitive information, and confidential reports. Collect only application information needed for selection; state who can access it, why it is collected, how long it is retained, and how applicants can request correction or deletion.

## 10. Review and activation checklist

Before this charter is treated as active:
- [ ] Project owner publishes approval and effective date.
- [ ] Community review period is completed and comments answered.
- [ ] Initial Board seat count, eligibility, terms, selection, removal, and succession rules are approved.
- [ ] Appointment panel and scoring rubric are published.
- [ ] Application intake and privacy notice are live and tested.
- [ ] Community ratification method and anti-manipulation limits are disclosed.
- [ ] Reporting templates and action register are available.
- [ ] Treasury policy is separately approved; any claimed multisig is independently verified.
- [ ] Public website and official channels link to the final charter and procedures.

**Until these steps are complete, this is a proposed operating framework—not a statement that a functioning Board, binding governance process, or multisig treasury system is already active.**
