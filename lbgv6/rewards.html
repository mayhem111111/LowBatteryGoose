COMMUNITY / REWARDS

Rewards, Airdrops & Staking

A community-first rewards framework for players, contributors, and the Low Battery Goose games. All programs remain planned until funding, rules, and wallet controls are verified.

Game prize pools

Future prize pools may support eligible players across Honk Bingo, Goose Trivia, Goose Guess, and future games. Each game will have published rules, eligibility, prize amounts, snapshots or scoring rules, and a public payout record before a token prize is offered.

Airdrops

Airdrops are planned as optional community rewards for verified participation and contributions. No airdrop is active or guaranteed. Eligibility, allocation, anti-bot checks, claim windows, and the funding wallet will be disclosed before any distribution.

Staking

Staking is a future development item only. There is no live staking contract, reward rate, or guaranteed yield. Any implementation must be separately designed, security-reviewed, funded, and publicly documented.

Treasury and prize funding

Game rewards, airdrops, and prize pools must be paid only from a disclosed, funded treasury allocation under the project's approved multisig controls. No player should send SOL or tokens to enter a free game or claim a reward.

Treasury deployment and investment policy

Any future use of treasury assets beyond approved operating expenses or game prizes requires a documented purpose, risk limits, governance approval, and public reporting. The project does not offer an investment product or promise returns. Treasury deployment, staking, and any related integrations remain proposals until separately approved, funded, and reviewed.
